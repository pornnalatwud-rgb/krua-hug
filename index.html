Real culture, in real homes.

Our platform connects tourists with local Thai people through authentic cultural experiences in local homes. Tourists can participate in hands-on activities such as cooking traditional Thai dishes, making Thai desserts, and creating Thai flower garlands (Phuang Malai).

Meaningful connections.

The platform aims to create meaningful connections between tourists and locals, allowing tourists to learn about Thai culture, traditions, and everyday life directly from local hosts in a warm and welcoming environment. At the same time, it provides locals with opportunities to share their skills, preserve Thai cultural heritage, and earn extra income.
